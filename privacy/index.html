Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Ada: Chess Coach (“the app”) is a chess app with an AI voice coach called Ada. It is made and run by Melim Guler, an individual developer in the Netherlands (“we”). We are responsible for your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at adachesscoach@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. The app has no advertising or analytics tools.
  • Your account is anonymous: no name, email or password.
  • Your voice is not recorded or stored. It is turned into text while you talk.
  • You choose how long the text of what you said is kept: up to 90 days, or not at all.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time in Settings.

Ada is an AI

Ada’s replies are written by an AI model, Claude by Anthropic, which runs for us on Amazon Bedrock in the EU. To write a reply, the model gets what you said, the position on the board and a short summary of your recent games. It is not used to train the model. A chess engine checks the facts in Ada’s replies, but she can still make mistakes, so treat her as a coach, not as the last word.

Transfers outside the EU

Most of your data stays in the EU. When a provider outside the EU or EEA processes it (RevenueCat, Expo, and Deepgram as a company), the transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as offered by that provider.

Our legal bases

  • To provide the app you asked for (contract): your account, games, lessons, coaching, plan and drills.
  • Your consent: using the microphone (you can turn it off at any time in your iPhone Settings and type instead) and adding a Chess.com or Lichess username.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and working, fair use, controlling costs, and reading the public games of a player you choose to analyse.

If someone analysed your games

Analyse a player reads only public, finished games that Chess.com or Lichess already publish. The report goes only to the person who asked for it, and it is deleted after 90 days. To have reports about your username removed sooner, or to ask what we hold, email us with your username and the site.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account and data. This removes your profile, progress, games and transcripts from our servers and from your phone. It does not cancel a subscription; do that in your iPhone Settings (see Support).
  • Delete transcripts: Settings → Delete all transcripts, or set Keep transcripts to Off.
  • Stop using your voice: turn off the microphone for the app in your iPhone Settings, or use Quiet mode and type.
  • Access, a copy, correction, restriction or objection: email us at adachesscoach@gmail.com. Because your account is anonymous, we will ask you for your account ID, which you can find in the app, so we only act on your own data. We reply within one month.
  • Complain: you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or to the authority where you live.

Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you think a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Security

All data travels encrypted (TLS). Each account can only read its own data, and the keys to our providers stay on our servers, never in the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters for how we use your data, we tell you in the app first.
