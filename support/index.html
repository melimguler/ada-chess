Ada: Chess Coach, the chess app with an AI voice coach

Write to us: adachesscoach@gmail.com

Tell us your iPhone model and the app version (Settings → Version; in the app, Settings is the icon at the top right of Today). For anything about your data, add your account ID (Settings → Account ID). We aim to reply within 3 working days, in English or Turkish.

Common questions

Ada can’t hear me

Allow the microphone: open your iPhone Settings → Apps → Chess Coach and turn on Microphone. In the app, check Settings → Microphone: with Push-to-talk on, hold the mic button while you speak. You can also use Quiet mode and type to Ada.

Ada talks over me

With earbuds, just start speaking and Ada stops. On the phone speaker, tap the coach bar to stop her. To hear less from her, set Settings → How much Ada talks to Quiet or Balanced.

What is Analyse a player?

Type a Chess.com or Lichess username and Ada checks that player's last 20 rapid, blitz and classical games: the openings they play with each colour, where they leave known opening theory (checked against the Lichess masters database), their strengths and weak spots, how to play against them, where their mistakes come from, their form, and positions where they went wrong, which you can practise from your side of the board. Only public games are read. Your first report is free; with a plan you get up to 5 a day.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language: English or Türkçe. Screens, lessons and Ada’s voice all change.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Ada: Chess Coach → Cancel Subscription. In the app, Settings → Plan takes you there too. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends so you are not charged again. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get my subscription back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the plans screen and tap Restore purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does Ada answer in text after many games?

Plans include up to 30 voice-coached games a month (fair use). After that, Ada keeps coaching you in text until the new month starts.

Can I move my progress to another phone?

Your account is anonymous (no email or password), so your progress belongs to the app on this phone. If you delete the app or change phones, it can’t be brought back. Your subscription can, with Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account and data deletes everything. Settings → Delete all transcripts deletes only the text of what you said. See the Privacy Policy.

Is my voice recorded?

No. Your voice is turned into text while you talk and is not stored. You choose how long the text is kept, or turn it off.

Ada got something wrong

A chess engine checks the facts in Ada’s replies, but she can still make mistakes. Send us a screenshot with the position and what she said, and we will look into it.
